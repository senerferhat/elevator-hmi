2. ROOT CAUSE (entirely on our side)
================================================================================

Two independent problems in our Linux host software, in sequence:

(a) **DSI initialisation was being sent at the wrong point in our display
    pipeline.** The Rockchip kernel we use switches the DSI host into VIDEO
    mode *before* it calls the panel driver's enable step. Our panel driver was
    sending your entire 196-register initialisation sequence (plus the page-4
    clock fix, Sleep Out and Display On) from that enable step -- meaning your
    initialisation commands were being transmitted as high-speed packets inside
    the blanking intervals of an already-running video stream, so the panel was
    receiving video data before it had ever been initialised.

    We moved the whole sequence earlier, into the "prepare" stage, where the
    host is still in low-power command mode and no video is streaming. This is
    the same order your fixture uses. **This was the actual fix.**

(b) **Our kernel had the framebuffer console disabled**, so although the
    display device existed, nothing was ever drawn into the framebuffer. An
    all-zero framebuffer is a correctly displayed black screen. This is why the
    panel still appeared "dead" to us for a further two weeks *after* fix (a)
    had already solved the real problem -- we were looking at a working panel
    faithfully displaying black. We have now enabled the framebuffer console.
