  - Backlight LEDA/LEDK: external bench supply (see below)

REWORK DISCLOSURE (important — the stock schematic does not describe this):
The carrier's VCC3V3_LCD load switch is physically defective on our unit: its
enable signal toggles correctly, but the switched output stays at ~0 V in both
enable polarities (DMM-verified). To proceed, we wired the panel 3.3 V input
directly from the board's main VCC3V3_SYS rail to CON1 pins 5/6, bypassing the
dead switch. Short jumper wire, no additional local capacitance added at the
connector. VDDIN measures 3.3 V steady-state by DMM. We do not currently have
an oscilloscope at this bench, so we cannot capture the transient behaviour of
this feed during boost start-up — relevant to questions B and C below.

Reset: XRES pulse measured/logged on every boot — high 5 ms, low 20 ms
(your Q1 diagram), high, then 120 ms before the first MIPI command.

Backlight: external supply on LEDA/LEDK (your spec: 9.6 V ±0.1 V, 180 mA).
LED string visibly lit during all tests — the "black" observations are with
glowing backlight, i.e. the glass is illuminated but shows no pixel content.

Power sequencing note: on this carrier the panel 3.3 V is present from board
power-on (direct rail), i.e. VDDIN is up long before the XRES pulse and first
command — consistent with the order in your Q1 timing diagram.
