================================================================================
1. NEW TEST — BIST WITH ZERO VIDEO EVER SENT (stricter than "independent of
   the host MIPI video stream")
================================================================================

Your 7 July email described BIST as running "independently of the host MIPI
video stream." We rebuilt our firmware so that is literally, completely true:
the entire sequence — init table, page-4 clock fix, Sleep Out, Display On,
and the BIST arm itself — now executes while our DSI host is still in
low-power COMMAND mode, before our host has ever switched to video mode at
all. We then held that state for a measured 2.02-second window (proof below)
before allowing video mode to start.

During that 2.02 s window, the panel had received precisely zero video data
of any kind, ever, since power-on. Result: unchanged — black, watched
continuously, no flash, no bars, no pattern.

Timing proof (verbatim target log, unit #1):

  [    3.624] jadard: entering command-mode init sequence (pre-video)
  [    3.624] jadard: init table sent (196 cmds)
  [    3.678] jadard: page-4 clock fix sent
  [    3.678] jadard: Sleep Out (0x11) sent
  [    3.804] jadard: Display On (0x29) sent
  [    3.764] jadard: BIST armed (F0,55/F1,AA/E0,01/E3,01)
  [    5.784] jadard: host switches to video mode <- first video ever sent

Gap from BIST-armed to video-mode switch: 2.02 seconds, matching our
intentional hold to the millisecond. Every command before that line
transacted over DSI low-power signaling only.

================================================================================
2. NEW TEST — 25x EXTENDED POWER-ON SETTLE TIME
================================================================================

Per your 11 May answer (Q2), MIPI commands must not be sent until VCC3V3_LCD
has stabilized at >=90% of rated value, with your Q1 diagram citing a 10 ms
delay. In case our rail's actual settle time differs from your fixture's, we
extended that delay to 250 ms — 25x your stated value — with everything else
unchanged. Result: unchanged — black.

================================================================================
3. WHAT THIS ADDS TO OUR 23 JULY REPORT
================================================================================

Neither test touched the init table, the command bytes, or anything you
specified — both changed only host-side timing/sequencing around the same
unmodified, byte-verified command stream. Combined with the 23 July result
(both panel units black under your originally-specified test), we believe
this now closes off the two most likely remaining host-side explanations
(video-mode interference, power settle time) for why BIST has never produced
a visible pattern on either of our units.

We remain very open to any further test you or your application engineer
would like us to run, and to shipping one or both units back for direct
inspection on your fixture.
