Dear Abby / LCD Mall FAE,

We have completed both tests from your June 5 reply.

TEST 1 RESULT — RK3566 MIPI Clock Fix:

We implemented your page-4 registers exactly as specified:
  {E0,04}, {0x37,0x58}, {0x35,0x08}, {0x36,0x49}, {0x2C,0x06}, {E0,00}
  inserted BEFORE 0x11 (Sleep Out), followed by 0x11 → 120ms → 0x29 → 5ms → 0x35,0x00.

Reading DCS register 0x0A (GET_POWER_MODE) just after DISPLAY_ON, we now see:

  BEFORE clock fix (our original init):    0x0A = 0x18
  AFTER  clock fix (TEST 1 image):         0x0A = 0x1c

The change from 0x18 → 0x1c shows that bit 2 (Display On / 0x04) is now SET.
The clock fix allowed DISPLAY_ON to be properly acknowledged by the panel.
This confirms the MIPI rate mismatch was real and your fix addressed it.

HOWEVER: Bit 7 (0x80) — the booster / internal charge pump — is still CLEAR
in both readings. A healthy JD9365D should read 0x9C (booster+display-on+normal+sleep-out).

FULL POWER_MODE 0x0A BIT DECODE:
  0x18 (before): booster=OFF, sleep-out=ON, normal=ON, display-on=OFF
  0x1c (after):  booster=OFF, sleep-out=ON, normal=ON, display-on=ON
  0x9C (target): booster=ON,  sleep-out=ON, normal=ON, display-on=ON

TEST 2 RESULT — BIST Self-Test:
